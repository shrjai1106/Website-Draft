FAQ

Questions schools ask us.

Have a different question? Call or WhatsApp 016-259 2727.

What ages do you teach?

We teach three levels: primary (ages 7 to 12), lower secondary (13 to 15) and upper secondary (16 to 17). Each level has its own pace, tools and projects.

Do students need their own laptops?

No. We can run sessions in your computer lab, bring equipment for robotics sessions, or host students at our Brickfields campus.

How do you keep AI lessons safe for children?

Every AI lesson covers safe and honest use first: privacy, checking facts and using AI to learn rather than to copy. Trainers use teacher-approved tools and accounts.

Are school programmes HRD Corp claimable?

Teacher upskilling programmes are HRD Corp claimable for schools registered with HRD Corp, including many private and international schools. Ask us about funding for student programmes.
