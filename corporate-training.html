Training & claim

Assess. Map. Train. Document. Claim.

Five steps from a skills gap to a funded, documented training run. We handle the training paperwork; you submit the grant application and the claim through your HRD Corp account.

  1. STEP 01

    Assess

    A short call with HR and team leads to pin down the skills gap, the tools in use and the outcome you need.

    You: tell us about the team and the goal.

  2. STEP 02

    Map

    We match an HRD Corp registered programme and tailor the outline and exercises to your industry.

    You: approve the outline and dates.

  3. STEP 03

    Train

    Hands-on delivery on your real use cases, with practical exercises and assessments in every module.

    You: apply for the grant before day one.

  4. STEP 04

    Document

    Attendance records, assessments, trainer details and a post-training report, prepared to HRD Corp requirements.

    We: send the full document pack.

  5. STEP 05

    Claim

    Submit the claim with our documents and recover the eligible cost from your HRD Corp levy.

    You: submit the claim. We answer any queries.

FAQ

Questions HR teams ask us.

Grant rules change from time to time. Check the latest guidelines in your HRD Corp portal, and we will help with anything course-related.

Are all PUMO courses HRD Corp claimable?

Yes. Every PUMO course is HRD Corp claimable for employers registered with HRD Corp. Our registered programmes run under SBL-Khas, and we prepare the course details and documents you need for the grant application and the claim.

What does HRD Corp claimable mean?

Malaysian employers registered with HRD Corp (formerly HRDF) pay a monthly levy. Training from a registered provider such as PUMO can be claimed against that levy, so the course is paid from funds your company has already set aside.

How far in advance should we apply?

Apply for the grant before the training starts. Contact us at least three to four weeks ahead so we can agree the outline, dates and trainer, and send the paperwork in time.

Can you train at our office or factory?

Yes. We deliver in-house anywhere in Malaysia, at our Brickfields training rooms, or live online. Travel and accommodation for sites outside the Klang Valley are quoted separately.

How does placement support work?

Share the roles you are hiring for. We shortlist certified PUMO learners whose portfolio matches, arrange interviews at your office or ours, and can run a train-and-hire cohort on your own tools before new hires start.

What group sizes do you train?

Corporate runs work well from about 5 to 25 participants per cohort. Larger teams are split into cohorts so everyone gets hands-on time.
